Shortcuts

  • SpacePlay / pause
  • ZAdd a zoom at the playhead
  • ⌫Delete the selection
  • EscDeselect / leave crop
  • ←→Step a frame (⇧ = 1 s)
  • ⌘+ scroll to zoom the timeline

Tips

  • Click a purple zoom to aim it. Drag the frame, drag a corner or scroll to zoom.
  • Drag clip edges to trim. Click a transition chip to change it.
  • Orange dots on the timeline are your clicks.
